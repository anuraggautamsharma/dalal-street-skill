# Disclaimer

**Please read this before using Dalal Street Skill.**

## 1. Education only, not advice

Dalal Street Skill ("this project") is free, open-source software for **learning about the stock market
and practising with paper (fake) money**. Its output (scans, chart readings, levels, plans, PASS/BLOCK
checks, simulated odds, news tags and paper-trade results) is **educational information and arithmetic**.
It is **not**:

- investment advice, research, a recommendation, a tip or a signal to buy, sell or hold any security;
- a solicitation or offer to buy or sell any security;
- a guarantee or prediction of any future price or return.

A **PASS** only means a plan follows the rules *you* configured. It does not mean the trade is good.

## 3. Trading is risky

- Investments in securities markets are subject to market risks. **You can lose some or all of your money.**
- Short-term and intraday trading is especially risky. SEBI's own studies found that a large majority of
  individual traders in the equity F&O segment lost money (about 9 in 10 in FY 2021-22), and that most
  individual intraday traders in the cash segment also lost money (about 7 in 10 in FY 2022-23).
- Past prices, backtests and simulations (including the Time Machine) do **not** predict the future.
  Results days, news, circuit limits, halts and gaps can do things no history shows.
- Paper-trading results are usually **better** than real results, which come with real slippage, real
  charges and real emotions.

## 4. Data and calculations may be wrong

- Market data comes from free, **unofficial** public sources (Yahoo Finance; NSE's public index lists). It
  may be delayed, incomplete, adjusted or wrong, and may change or stop working without notice.
- Brokerage, taxes and fees are **estimates** based on published rates that change over time. Your
  broker's contract note is the only true record of your charges.
- Always verify prices and charges in your own broker's app before making any decision.

## 5. No orders, no credentials

This project **cannot and does not place orders** with any broker, and it never needs your broker login,
password, PIN, OTP or API keys. Never share these with anyone, including any AI assistant. Every real
trade is your own decision, placed by you, in your own account.

## 6. Beware of tips and scams

Be careful with anyone (on WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, YouTube or elsewhere) who promises guaranteed
returns, sells "sure-shot tips", or asks you to transfer money or share your login. SEBI regularly warns
investors about unregistered advisers and "finfluencers".

speculative business income). Nothing here is tax advice. Consult a chartered accountant.
